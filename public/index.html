Eagle Creek Golf Club combines scenic views with a championship-level course that offers a memorable golfing experience. Nestled amidst lush landscapes and serene water features, this course is known for its challenging design and exceptional playing conditions, making it a must-play destination for golf enthusiasts. Designed to challenge every level of player, the layout winds through mature oaks, native wetlands and rolling fairways, where well-placed bunkers and water hazards reward thoughtful shot-making. Beyond the course, guests enjoy a full-service clubhouse, a driving range and putting greens, and warm Florida hospitality that makes Eagle Creek an unforgettable stay-and-play destination.

When is the Best Time of Year to Play at Eagle Creek Golf Club?

Fall and Spring are the best seasons to play at Eagle Creek Golf Club. During these months, the weather is mild with minimal rain, providing perfect golfing conditions. Winter is also a great option for those seeking year-round play in Florida's warmer climate.
